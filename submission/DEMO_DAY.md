# SF Demo Day (Oct 8): pitch + checklist

## 60-second pitch
> "When someone you love is far away, a text feels small. Capy Post lets you send them a *world*.
>
> I tell it who it's for: my friend Sam, who just moved across the world. Three things Sam loves: a cake, a cat, and a little red bicycle I'll type in. While **Tripo** sculpts the bicycle live, a crew of capybaras hauls crates onto the island. *[crate pops]*
>
> I wrap it and get a link. Here's what Sam sees: a present. Tap it, the box falls open, and the world grows out of it. Our host capybara is a Tripo model too, auto-rigged as a biped, so it waves while the letter unfolds. The cat was rigged as a quadruped, so it actually walks.
>
> Every capybara and gift here was generated, rigged and animated with Tripo. Every gift is a share, and every recipient is one tap from sending one back. That's the growth loop.
>
> Capy Post: send someone a tiny world."

## Live demo order (about 2 min)
1. Open `/#/create` → **Fill an example** (1 typed item + 2 shelf items, so it only spends one live generation).
2. **Have the capybaras build it**. Talk over the crate-hauling for ~90s, or open a pre-made gift while it runs.
3. **Wrap it up** → **Preview their unwrapping** → tap the box.
4. Close the letter, drag to orbit, poke a capybara, tap a gift label.
5. Show the moon and kid worlds via the "Peek inside" chips on the landing page.
